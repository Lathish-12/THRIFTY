# 🎯 3 CLICKS TO ADD DATABASE

## Just do these 3 things:

### 👆 CLICK 1: The "+ Create" Button
**Location:** Top right corner of Railway screen
**What to click:** The button that says `+ Create` or `+ New`

---

### 👆 CLICK 2: "Database"
**What you'll see:** A menu pops up
**What to click:** Click on `Database`

---

### 👆 CLICK 3: "PostgreSQL"
**What you'll see:** List of database types
**What to click:** Click on `PostgreSQL`

---

## ✅ Done!

Railway will now:
- Create a PostgreSQL database (takes 30 seconds)
- Automatically connect it to your backend
- Add `DATABASE_URL` to your environment variables

You'll see **2 boxes** on your screen:
1. THRIFTY (backend)
2. PostgreSQL (database)

**That's it!** 🎉
