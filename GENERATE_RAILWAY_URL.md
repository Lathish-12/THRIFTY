# 🌐 HOW TO GENERATE YOUR RAILWAY PUBLIC URL

**Problem:** No public URL exists yet - Railway doesn't create one automatically!
**Solution:** Generate it manually (takes 30 seconds!)

---

## 🎯 STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS

You're currently on the **THRIFTY** service Settings page. Here's what to do:

### Step 1: Find the Networking Section

On the Settings page you're on right now:

1. **Scroll down** on that same page
2. Look for a section called **"Networking"**
3. You might also see **"Public Networking"** or **"Domains"**

---

### Step 2: Generate Domain

In the Networking section, you should see:

**Option A: If you see a button "Generate Domain"**
- Click **"Generate Domain"** button
- Railway will create a public URL instantly!
- It will look like: `https://web-production-xxxx.up.railway.app`
- **COPY THIS URL!**

**Option B: If you see "Add a Public Domain"**
- Click **"Add a Public Domain"** or just **"+"** 
- Railway will auto-generate a `.railway.app` domain
- **COPY THIS URL!**

**Option C: If the section says "No public networking"**
- Look for a button to **enable** or **add** public networking
- Click it to generate the domain

---

### Step 3: Copy the URL

Once generated, you'll see something like:
```
https://thrifty-production-abc123.up.railway.app
```
OR
```
https://web-production-xyz789.up.railway.app
```

**Copy the ENTIRE URL and paste it to me!**

## ⚠️ CAN'T FIND NETWORKING SECTION?

### Alternative Method 1: Check Overview Tab

1. Click **"Overview"** tab (next to Settings)
2. Look for **"Domains"** or **"Public URL"** section
3. Click **"Generate Domain"** if you see it

### Alternative Method 2: Check Deployments Tab

1. Click **"Deployments"** tab
2. Click on the latest successful deployment
3. Look for domain/URL information there

## 🚀 WHY YOU NEED THIS URL

This public URL is how your **frontend** (Vercel) will talk to your **backend** (Railway).

Without it:
- ❌ Frontend can't make API calls
- ❌ Login won't work
- ❌ No data can be saved
- ❌ AI Advisor won't work

With it:
